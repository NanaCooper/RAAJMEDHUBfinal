# RAAJ MEDHUB B2B Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

At RAAJ MEDHUB, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our partners, service providers, and users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use the RAAJ MEDHUB B2B scheduling and referral coordination application (the "Service").

Please read this policy carefully. By accessing or using our Service, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To facilitate efficient service coordination and scheduling between B2B partners, we collect the following types of information:

- **Account Profile Information**: Name, business email address, phone number, and account credentials.
- **Provider & Organization Information**: Professional role, qualifications, and organization/entity names in our provider directory.
- **Scheduling & Coordination Data**: Appointment records, service details, dates, times, and B2B partner connection references to coordinate professional tasks.
- **Minimal Technical Logs**: IP addresses, crash reports, device identifiers, and platform logs to ensure the Service remains stable and secure.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly to deliver, optimize, and support our professional B2B scheduling network, including:

- Managing and coordinating appointment bookings and service schedules between active partners.
- Enabling the B2B provider directory so members can easily locate and contact network partners.
- Sending system updates, reminders, and service notifications to active users.
- Identifying, debugging, and resolving technical crashes or performance drops.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Security, and Integrity

We prioritize the security of your professional collaboration data:

- **Secure Infrastructure**: All database records are securely hosted using industrial-grade cloud encryption in transit and at rest.
- **Access Control**: Active user sessions are encrypted locally on your device using secure local storage.
- **Data Minimization**: We collect only the minimal information required to facilitate coordination between our service providers and partners.

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared exclusively to support your scheduling workflows:

- **Between Active Partners**: Scheduling details and directory listings are visible to authorized B2B network members to facilitate coordination.
- **Compliance & Protection**: We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legitimate legal requests.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Controls

Under active platform guidelines, you retain full ownership and control over your account data:

- **Access & Correction**: You can review and edit your professional profile directly through the App settings page.
- **Data Deletion**: You can request the permanent deletion of your account and related data at any time via the Settings menu inside the App.
