# MediCare App Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 2026-03-31**

At MediCare, we are committed to protecting your personal health information (PHI). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

## Permissions We Request

MediCare may request the following device permissions only to support app features:

- **Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)**: Used to scan and capture medical referrals/documents if you choose to take a photo from within the app.
- **Photos/Media (Photo Library / Storage access)**: Used to let you select and upload existing images/documents from your device (e.g., Android media/storage permissions).
- **Microphone (if enabled in the app)**: Used only for voice notes if you choose to record them.
- **Notifications**: Used to send appointment reminders and other service notifications (you can disable notifications in device settings).
- **Vibration**: Used only to provide haptic feedback for notifications.
- **Internet/Network access**: Required for core app functionality such as signing in, syncing your data, and securely communicating with Firebase services.

You can deny these permissions; some features may not work without them.

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Profile Data**: Your name, age, phone number, and email.
- **Medical Documentation**: Images of referrals and medical records that you upload.
- **Usage Data**: We collect minimal technical data via Firebase for app performance and crash reports.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To manage and schedule your medical appointments.
- To provide doctors with necessary medical history for your consults.
- To send appointment reminders via notifications.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- **Firebase Encryption**: All data is stored using Google’s Firebase infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest.
- **Local Security**: Sensitive session identifiers are stored using Expo Secure Store on your device.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal/clinical obligations. You can request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to any legal requirements).

## 5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of your medical data or ask for the deletion of your account at any time via the settings menu in the MediCare app.

## 6. Third-Party Sharing

We **do not** sell your medical data to third parties. Data is shared only with the medical professionals at your chosen branch.
