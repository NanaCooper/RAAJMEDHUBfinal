# MediCare App Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 18, 2026**

At MediCare, we are committed to protecting your personal health information (PHI). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

## Permissions We Request
MediCare may request the following device permissions only to support app features:

- **Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)**: Used to scan and capture medical referrals/documents if you choose to take a photo from within the app.
- **Photos/Media (Photo Library / Storage access)**: Used to let you select and upload existing images/documents (such as medical referral letters) from your device.
- **Notifications**: Used to send appointment reminders, scan updates, and other service notifications (you can disable notifications in your device settings).
- **Vibration**: Used only to provide haptic feedback for app notifications.
- **Internet/Network access**: Required for core app functionality such as signing in, syncing your data, and securely communicating with Firebase services.

You can deny these permissions; however, some features (such as uploading your request form) may not work without them.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Profile Data**: Your name, age, phone number, and email.
- **Medical Documentation**: Images of referrals and medical records that you choose to upload.
- **Usage Data**: We collect minimal technical data via Firebase for app performance and crash reports to ensure app stability.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To manage and schedule your medical appointments.
- To provide healthcare professionals at our branches with the necessary medical history for your consults and diagnostic procedures.
- To send appointment reminders and scheduling updates via notifications.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Firebase Encryption**: All data is stored using Google's secure Firebase infrastructure with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.
- **Local Security**: Sensitive session identifiers are stored securely on your device using hardware-backed local storage (Expo SecureStore).

## 4. Data Retention
We retain your data only as long as needed to provide the clinic services and to meet legal/clinical obligations. You can request the deletion of your account and associated data (subject to any clinic regulatory records retention requirements).

## 5. Your Rights
You can request a copy of your medical data or ask for the deletion of your account at any time via the settings menu in the MediCare app.

## 6. Third-Party Services & Sharing
- **Authentication**: We use Google Sign-In as an identity provider. When you sign in using your Google account, their use of your information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
- **Medical Staff**: We **do not** sell or lease your medical data to third parties. Data is shared exclusively with authorized medical professionals at your chosen branch (Koforidua, Takoradi, or Cape Coast) to facilitate your care.
